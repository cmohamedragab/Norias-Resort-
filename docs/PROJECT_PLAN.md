# Norias Resort PMS
## Project Plan

### 1. General
- Resort: Norias Resort, Fayoum, Egypt.
- Platforms: Android and Windows.
- Default language: Arabic.
- Optional language: English.
- Arabic RTL and English LTR.

### 2. User Roles
- Manager: Full access.
- Reception: Reservations and room operations.
- Accountant: Accounting and cashbox.

### 3. Main Modules
- Reservations and guests.
- Rooms and room status.
- Check-in and check-out.
- Cashbox and accounting.
- Pool tickets and trips.
- Photoshoot bookings.
- Restaurant and kitchen.
- Inventory and purchases.
- Employees and attendance.
- Maintenance.
- Guest complaints.
- Reports and statistics.
- Users and permissions.

### 4. Important Requirements
- Flexible manually entered deposits.
- Payment methods: Cash, Visa, InstaPay, Bank Transfer.
- Detailed transaction history.
- Audit log for employee actions.
- Cloud backup and offline support.
- Excel and PDF reports.
- Future online booking support.
